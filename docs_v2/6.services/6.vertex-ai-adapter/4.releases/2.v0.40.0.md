# DIAL Vertex AI Adapter 0.40.0

DIAL Vertex AI Adapter 0.40.0 upgrades the DIAL SDK; its patches upgrade dependencies only. This page summarizes the release and its patch releases, 0.40.1 and 0.40.2, with a link to the full notes of each. It is for platform engineers who plan an adapter upgrade.

Platform release 1.48 ships DIAL Vertex AI Adapter 0.40.0; see [Upgrade to DIAL 1.48](../../../8.reference/changelog/upgrade-to-1.48.md).

| [0.40.1](#0401) | 2026-10-01 | Patch |
| [0.40.2](#0402) | 2026-10-05 | Patch |

## 0.40.0

Released 2026-09-22. [Release notes](https://github.com/epam/ai-dial-adapter-vertexai/releases/tag/0.40.0)

- **Changed:** `aidial-sdk` upgraded from 0.39.0 to 0.42.0.

## Patch releases

### 0.40.1

Released 2026-10-01. [Release notes](https://github.com/epam/ai-dial-adapter-vertexai/releases/tag/0.40.1)

- **Changed:** dependency upgrades, including `aidial-adapter-anthropic` 0.17.1, `oauthlib` 4.0.0, `pyjwt` 2.14.0, and `urllib3` 2.8.0.

### 0.40.2

Released 2026-10-05. [Release notes](https://github.com/epam/ai-dial-adapter-vertexai/releases/tag/0.40.2)

- **Changed:** dependency upgrades, including `pyjwt` 2.15.0 and `pypdf` 6.19.0.
